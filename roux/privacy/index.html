PRIVACY

Your data stays with you.

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Roux does not collect, track, sell, or send your personal data anywhere. Your recipes, meal plans, shopping list, settings, and recipe photos are stored locally on your device.

No accounts or tracking

Roux does not require an account and does not use analytics, advertising trackers, or remote servers. We do not receive a copy of anything you create in the app.

Exports and sharing

When you choose to export a backup or share a meal plan, your device presents its system share sheet. You choose where that file goes; Roux does not receive or retain a copy.

Contact

If you have a question about this policy, email roux@lmcjt.dev.
