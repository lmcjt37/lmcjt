SUPPORT

How can we help?

For help with Roux, feedback, or a problem with the app, send an email and include as much detail as you can.

roux@lmcjt.dev

Useful details to include

  • What you were trying to do.
  • What happened instead.
  • Your device model and iOS version.
  • A screenshot, if you can safely share one.

Your recipes are local

Roux stores your data on your device. If you need to move it, use the in-app export and import tools in Settings → Data management.
