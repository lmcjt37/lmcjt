Tiny Delight Studies

Flourishes that earn their place.

These studies explore what happens when product polish becomes part of the experience rather than decoration pasted on top. The goal is to find motion and surprise that clarify intent.

  • Uses feedback to reinforce actions, not distract from them.
  • Explores hidden states and playful transitions with restraint.
  • Connects visual detail to user understanding.
Talk about interface craft Loop back to project one

Why it matters

Memorable products are often won by small details. The trick is making those details support the task rather than compete with it.

What it proves

I can work at the level of motion, pacing, and tactile feedback without losing the broader product story.

Motion language

A good transition should explain where you are going, what changed, and why the system still feels coherent.

Design principle

Delight is stronger when it is discovered. That is why these studies emphasize optional curiosity over mandatory spectacle.
