**Slop grenade** meaning:

> Generally refers to a large piece of writing or code that someone has generated using AI and without reading it just throws it to someone else to read or review it

There is even a dedicated website [here](https://noslopgrenade.com/) which is the new [lmgtfy](https://lmgtfy.com/) that you can share with all your colleagues when they throw you one.

The term "slop grenade" was actually coined in a video interview on the Knowledge Project with the Shopify founder and CEO Tobi Lütke, which you can watch [here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G9P9D9hptq8).
