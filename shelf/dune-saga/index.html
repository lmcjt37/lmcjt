Dune saga

I came to Dune looking for something rooted in sci-fi and worth sinking into, without really knowing the history behind it, the scale of the saga, or how much canon sat around it. What hooked me straight away was the world building: the lore, the factions, and the sense that almost everything on the page carries more weight than it first lets on.

The further I got into the series, the more I started to notice how much Dune has fed into other stories, films, and shows. You can see the building blocks of so much modern sci-fi in it. For me, the strongest run is still Dune and Messiah, following Paul Atreides. After that it drifts a little before pulling things back together again in Chapterhouse.

I would absolutely recommend at least the first books. They stand on their own, and now they also make a great companion to the recent Denis Villeneuve films, which have done a strong job of bringing that world to a wider audience.
